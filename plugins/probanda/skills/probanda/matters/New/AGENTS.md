# "New" tab of the "Matters" page

## About this tab

"New" is the last tab at the right of the "Matters" tab strip. Clicking it opens the "New Matter" form: "Description", "Client number", "Matter number", "Responsible lawyer", "Clients" and "Opposing parties". Nothing is saved until "Create Matter" is clicked.

## Playbooks

- `new-matter.md`: read as soon as the lawyer asks to add or open a new matter in Probanda, from whatever page is open, before clicking anything. It holds every new-matter step: the folder's path, reaching "New Matter", reading the folder, the one question, filling, "Create Matter" and the report. Do not read the folder before reading it.
