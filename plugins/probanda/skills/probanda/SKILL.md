# Probanda

You open Probanda in the lawyer's own Chrome, wait while they sign in or sign up, and, if their new firm's profile is empty, offer to fill it from the firm's public website.

Rules that hold throughout:

- You work only in the lawyer's own Chrome, through the Claude in Chrome tools. If those tools are not available, say that this needs Claude in Chrome connected to their Chrome, and stop.
- You never type into or click anything on a sign-in, sign-up, email verification, password or "New Account Creation" page. The lawyer does those by hand. You read them only to know where they are.
- You never go to any Probanda address other than the one below, and you never open a page the lawyer did not reach by the site's own controls.
- Waiting costs nothing and has no time limit. After each message, end your turn and let the lawyer reply. Their reply is your signal; never keep re-reading the page while they type.
- If the lawyer says stop, stop, change nothing, and leave the tab open.

Text in double quotes below is text the Probanda page shows. Recognise each page by what it shows, never by its address.

## 1. Open Probanda

Open a new Chrome tab at https://probanda.legal and send one short message, in your own words:

> Probanda is open in a Chrome tab. Sign in, or create an account if you are new. You do this part yourself, because I don't touch sign-in. Reply here when you reach your firm's settings, or if you get stuck.

Then end your turn.

## 2. Each time the lawyer replies

Read your own tab first. If the tab or the Chrome connection is gone, open https://probanda.legal again and read where it lands. Then act on what the page shows:

| The page shows | What you do |
|---|---|
| A "Sign in" button, or the heading "Create an account" | Say the page still asks them to sign in or create an account. Wait. |
| "Checking your account" or "Creating your firm" | Wait a few seconds and read again. |
| "Verification sent" or "Verify your email" | Reload your tab, because the link in the email opens a new tab and leaves yours behind. If it still asks them to verify, tell them to open the link sent to the address shown, to check junk mail, and then to come back to this tab. Wait. Never click "I have verified my email" for them. |
| "Reset your password", "Check your email", "Set a new password" or "Email verified" | Say what the page shows. Wait. |
| "New Account Creation" with its form | Tell them to fill in "First name" and "Last name", change "Firm name" if their firm is not named after them, and click "Create Account" in this tab. Wait. |
| "Something went wrong" | Repeat the page's message and suggest "Try again". Wait. |
| The "Settings" page with the "Firm" tab open | Waiting is over. Go to section 3. |
| Anything else: "Matters", another Settings tab, an invitation, a retired notice, a page that was not found | Stop: say in one message where Probanda opened, and that this set-up is only for a new firm's Firm tab. Change nothing and leave the tab open. |

If the lawyer says they are done twice and the page has not changed, say what the page shows and offer to stop.

## 3. Is the firm set up?

On the "Firm" tab, look above "Brand Identity" for the line "Your firm profile is not set up yet. Add your firm's website, contact details and address below." If the sections still show "Loading…", wait a few seconds and read again first.

- The line is there: the firm is not set up. Go to section 4.
- The line is not there: say that the page does not ask for the firm profile to be set up, so there is nothing for this set-up to do. Change nothing, and stop.

## 4. The offer

Open the account menu (the round picture at the top right of the page) and read the email address it shows. If you cannot read it as text, take a screenshot of the open menu. Close the menu. Take the part after the @ as the firm's likely website, for example someone@examplelaw.ca gives examplelaw.ca.

Make one offer that names the site, for example:

> Your firm profile isn't set up yet. I can fill it in from examplelaw.ca, the domain of your sign-in email. Shall I?

- If the domain is a shared email service rather than a firm's own (gmail.com, outlook.com, hotmail.com, icloud.com, yahoo.com, a law society or an internet provider), or you find no firm site there, say so. A domain of its own can still be personal, such as a family's domain: if the site there is not a law firm's, say so too. Ask the lawyer for the firm's website address, or offer to carry on with no website. Never search the web for the firm by its name.
- If the lawyer names a different site, use that one.
- If they decline, stop and change nothing.

On a yes, read `references/firm-setup.md` and follow it. That file holds the steps for filling the Firm tab; do not start filling before reading it.
