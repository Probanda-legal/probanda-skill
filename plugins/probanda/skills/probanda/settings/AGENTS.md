# "Settings" page

## Glossary

- Member: a person on the "Members" tab. A member with no tick under "Licensed" has no sign-in yet.
- "Owner": the member who created the firm. Always an administrator.
- "Administrator": a member who may change the "Firm", "Members" and "Governance" tabs.
