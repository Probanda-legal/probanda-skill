# "Members" tab of the "Settings" page

## About this tab

The firm's people. Everyone in the firm sees the list. Only an administrator sees "Add Member" and the buttons in "Actions"; others see "Only firm administrators can change this". "Add Member" records a person and gives no access. "Invite" makes a link the lawyer sends from their own email, because Probanda sends none. A tick under "Licensed" means the person has their own sign-in.

## Playbooks

- `members-setup.md`: read whenever this tab is open after sign-in. It holds the whole members set-up: the check that the lawyer may add members, the offer, reading the firm's site for its people, adding them and the report. Do not make the offer or add anyone before reading it.
