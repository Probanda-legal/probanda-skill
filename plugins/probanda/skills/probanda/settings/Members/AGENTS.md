# "Members" tab of the "Settings" page

## About this tab

The firm's people. Everyone in the firm sees the list. Only an administrator sees "Add Member" and the buttons in "Actions"; others see "Only firm administrators can change this". "Add Member" records a person and gives no access. "Invite" makes a link the lawyer sends from their own email, because Probanda sends none. A tick under "Sign-in" means the person has their own sign-in. "Licence" shows a lawyer's last check in the Law Society of BC directory: a tick and the date, or a cross, the date and the status the directory showed.

## Playbooks

- `members-setup.md`: read whenever this tab is open after sign-in. It holds the whole members set-up: the check that the lawyer may add members, reading the firm's site for its people, adding them and the report. It adds without asking first and reports afterwards. Do not add anyone before reading it. It never checks a licence.
- `licence-check.md`: read when the lawyer asks to check, verify or confirm one lawyer's licence or standing. It holds the directory search, how to tell the entry is the member, recording the result and the report.
